The brand

NUMEROLOGIE · Numerolog1e

An independent brand from the South of France, founded in 2018 by Cédric Chaudet. Designed in France. Manufactured in China. Built on one principle: no two pieces are the same.

Founder

Cédric Chaudet

Designer & founder. Former designer at Façonnable; founder of Vestiaires. In 2018 he created NUMEROLOGIE as a more personal and creative brand — « une marque de cœur » (a brand from the heart), rooted in the light of the Midi. His motto: « Chaque pièce est unique » — every piece is unique.
