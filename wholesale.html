Territory & Representation

Territories, agreed individually

Territories are agreed individually according to the representative’s existing retailer relationships, market coverage and development plan. Any exclusivity is subject to mutually agreed territory boundaries, duration, performance expectations and account responsibilities. Additional markets or national accounts may be negotiated separately.

Ordering & Delivery

Seasonal, order-based wholesale

Numerolog1e operates a seasonal, order-based wholesale model, with production consolidated across participating retail partners. Order deadlines, minimum quantities, payment terms and estimated delivery windows are confirmed for each collection. Our distribution model is designed to support reliable delivery to individual retail partners.

Designed in the South of France. Manufactured in China. Retail partners we have in mind include independent boutiques, specialty menswear stores, and selected contemporary retailers.
